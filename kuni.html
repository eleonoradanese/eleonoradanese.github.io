Kuni

2022 — today

Kuni is Eleonora and she writes songs in Rome.

She started writing in 2019 on an electric piano she didn't know how to play, with some background in music studies and the classic high school punk band behind her. In 2023 she released her debut EP, "Walls Have Told Me", produced by Golden Years. In 2024 she chose to collaborate with Giacomo Greco (Inude) on three more singles: "My Best", "Showu" and "Supposed To Be".

Over those years, critics responded warmly to Kuni's music, with outlets such as SkyTg24, DLSO, RockIT, Earmilk, Glamglare, Indie For Bunnies, Seituttolindie and many others following her project and appreciating its storytelling and sound.

For a while, work and personal life took precedence over her songwriting — but in 2026 Kuni returned to the studio, this time with Fabio Grande. Together they are working on her debut album, due out in early 2027, preceded by three singles: "She", "Francesca" and "Ex Lovers Club". All of Kuni's music has been released through Factory Flaws.
