Kuni

2022 — today

Kuni — short for Kunimitsu, one of the main characters from the video game Tekken — writes songs in Rome. She had a punk-rock band in high school where she sang and sometimes played the guitar, but she started writing her first songs only in 2019, and they immediately felt like a nice starting point: a well-balanced combination of her artistic influences, putting together sincere lyrics, folk-pop melodies, and punk-rock chords. Kuni released her first EP, "Walls Have Told Me", via Factory Flaws in June 2023. Three more singles followed during 2024: "My Best", "Showu", and "Supposed To Be". 2026 is the year when Kuni will release new songs, which will anticipate her first full-length album, coming out in the autumn.
